About compile/

Exam prep for Caribbean students, written by a Jamaican IT and computer science teacher.

Who runs it

compile/ is built and maintained by Phil, an Information Technology and Computer Science teacher in Jamaica. Lessons follow the CSEC Information Technology and CAPE Computer Science syllabuses, and every quiz question comes with an explanation of why the right answer is right.

What you get

  • Lessons organised by syllabus unit
  • Practice quizzes at three levels, plus timed mock exams
  • Flashcards that bring missed questions back at the right time
  • Study plans built from your exam date, and progress tracking with XP and streaks
  • A binary converter that shows its working

It is free to use. The site is supported by ads. compile/ is not affiliated with CXC.

Contact

Found a mistake in a lesson or question, have a suggestion, or want to ask about using compile/ with your class? Email tutorcole15@gmail.com. Please say which lesson or quiz you mean.
