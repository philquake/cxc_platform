Which device converts a computer's digital signal into an analogue signal for a telephone line?

A modem modulates digital data into an analogue signal to send it, and demodulates it at the other end. The name is short for modulator-demodulator. A router forwards packets between networks, and switches and hubs connect devices inside one network.
