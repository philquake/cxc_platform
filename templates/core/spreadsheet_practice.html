Write the formula for the highlighted cell. It is checked on your data and on changed data, so typing the answer in does not pass.

Challenge

Functions you can use

SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, COUNTIF, SUMIF, IF, AND, OR, ROUND, ABS and VLOOKUP, with the operators + - * / ^ & and comparisons. Use $ for absolute references, as in $B$2.

Relative and absolute references

Copying =B2*C2 down one row gives =B3*C3. A reference written $C$1 does not move when copied.

COUNTIF and SUMIF

=COUNTIF(B2:B7,">5") counts cells matching a condition. =SUMIF(C2:C7,">3",B2:B7) adds the cells in the last range where the first range matches.
