This Privacy Policy explains what information compile/ ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use our exam-prep platform, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using compile/, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: username and password when you sign up. We store your password in hashed form and never in plain text.
  • Profile information: an optional display name, if you choose to set one.

Information generated by your use of the Service

  • Learning activity: lessons you view, whether you mark a lesson complete, quiz attempts, selected answers, scores, and timestamps.
  • Progress and gamification data: XP totals, streaks, and topic leaderboard standing.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) collected automatically for security and troubleshooting.
  • Cookies: a session cookie to keep you signed in, and a CSRF-protection cookie required for secure form submission. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you when you sign in;
  • To operate core features: tracking lesson completion, scoring quizzes, calculating XP and streaks, and displaying topic leaderboards;
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service, including detecting abuse;
  • To communicate with you about your account or material changes to the Service;
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used so we can improve lessons, quizzes, and site design.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve third-party advertising.

3. Leaderboards and visibility to other users

Your username and XP total may be visible to other signed-in users on topic and subject leaderboards. No other account or progress information (such as your quiz answers, lesson history, or account credentials) is shown to other users.

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who host or operate infrastructure on our behalf (for example, our hosting and database providers), under obligations to protect your data;
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of compile/, our users, or the public;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, in which case we will take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to be protected.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and learning-activity data for as long as your account is active, so that your lesson progress, quiz history, and XP remain available to you. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or legitimate administrative purposes.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can review and update your account information at any time while signed in.
  • Deletion: you may request that we delete your account and associated personal data by contacting us at the address below.
  • Leaderboard visibility: if you would prefer your username not appear on leaderboards, contact us and we will look into options available at the time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information under local data protection law. We will honor applicable requests to the extent required.

7. Children's privacy

compile/ is designed to support secondary and post-secondary exam preparation and may be used by students under 18. We do not knowingly collect more information from a minor than is needed to operate the account and learning features described above (a username, a password, and study activity). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information you did not consent to, please contact us and we will address it.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls on our infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International use

compile/ is built primarily for students across the Caribbean. Depending on where our hosting and database infrastructure is located, your information may be processed or stored outside your home country. We take steps to protect your information wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the Service.
